# Why Smart Homes Fail: It’s Not Devices, It’s Architecture

Most smart home failures do not begin with bad devices.
They begin with fragmented system design.

## What usually goes wrong

- Too many disconnected platforms
- Wi-Fi never designed for automation load
- Cloud dependencies introduced without risk analysis
- No clear system owner or architecture plan

## Why this fails over time

A home can tolerate isolated device issues.
It usually cannot tolerate architectural inconsistency.

## Our approach

We treat the home as a system, not a collection of products.

## Key principles

- local-first where possible
- structured network backbone
- unified system logic
- reduced vendor lock-in
